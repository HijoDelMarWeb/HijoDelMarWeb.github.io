How It Works

A simple, direct process tailored entirely to you. No endless forms, no waiting. Just you, your question and a captain with 30 years at sea.

The process, step by step
1
Get in touch and describe your situation

Write via WhatsApp, email or the contact form on the site. You don't need to have everything figured out — just tell me what's on your mind: planning a voyage, reviewing your safety gear, buying a boat, or a nautical question you're not sure how to tackle.

WhatsApp +56 9 2395 6367 contacto@hijodelmar.cl Contact form
2
We assess your query and agree on the format

I respond personally — no bots, no intermediaries. We discuss whether the advisory will be via WhatsApp, video call or email; how long it will take; and a fair price for what you need. Some questions are resolved in a quick exchange; others require a more in-depth session.

Online · In person All Chile English available
3
We carry out the advisory session

The session focuses entirely on your case: we analyse your route, your vessel, your equipment or the purchase you're considering. I ask precise questions, give you my honest professional judgment and answer your questions one by one. Not a generic script — real nautical expertise applied to your actual situation.

Route analysis Equipment review Purchase evaluation Voyage planning
4
You receive a clear, actionable summary

When we finish, you get a summary of the key points: what to do, what to avoid, what to buy or how to prepare. No unnecessary jargon — just what you need to make good decisions at sea. And if a follow-up question comes up, I'm there to answer it.

Written summary Concrete recommendations Follow-up available
What type of advisory do you need?
Navigation Planning

Route, weather, tides, anchorages and crew preparation analysis for your voyage.
Safety Equipment

Review of lifejackets, EPIRB, liferaft, VHF and all emergency equipment on board.
Electronic Aids

GPS, AIS, autopilot, chartplotter and communications equipment on board.
Survival at Sea

Emergency protocols, vessel abandonment, distress signals and liferaft management.
Boat Purchase Help

Listing evaluation, hidden risk detection and guidance before you buy.
