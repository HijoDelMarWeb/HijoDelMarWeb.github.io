Marinas of Chile: Where to Berth Your Sailboat

Directory of marinas and yacht clubs in Chile, north to south, with address and contact, for sailors arriving by sailboat.

By Captain Rodrigo Troncoso · Updated November 2026 · 8 min read

If you plan to sail to Chile, or along its coast, you need to know where to tie up. This guide gathers the marinas and yacht clubs with berths that appear in cruising directories and Directemar documents, listed from north to south, with their address and the contact details I found. It is not an official or complete list, and much of the data comes from directories that are years old: call or write before you arrive and confirm berth availability, maximum length and fees.

Before you arrive: foreign recreational boaters must request written authorisation to sail Chilean inland waters from the Maritime Authority at the first port of arrival, follow the rules it sets and carry charts of the area (General Regulation on Nautical Sports, art. 36). Vessels departing from or arriving from abroad are governed by the Reception and Dispatch of Vessels Regulation (art. 33). More in the zarpe guide and the sailing in Chile guide.

Arica and Parinacota

Club de Deportes Náuticos de Arica

Arica
  • Address: Former Isla Alacrán area, no street number
  • Phone: +56 9 9541 3139
  • Email: Clubnauticoarica1@gmail.com

According to Noonsite: 70 berths for boats up to 18 m, with power, 24-hour security and a hardstand. Directemar asks arriving yachts to give their ETA on VHF 16 to Arica Radio.

Source: Directemar (April 2025)

No reliable data

For these marinas and clubs I could not find a reliable address or phone number: Club de Yates Iquique, Marina Las Tacas (Coquimbo), Marina Puerto Aldea (Tongoy), Club de Yates Papudo, Yacht Club Algarrobo, Don Manu (Llanquihue), Deportivo Phoenix (Valdivia) and the marinas on Lake Llanquihue and Puerto Varas. For them, the safest option is to ask at the local Capitanía de Puerto.

Contact details are not guaranteed. Sources: Directemar resolutions (2022 to 2025), Noonsite, PredictWind and Portbooker. Phone numbers marked "old format" have fewer digits than the current numbering and may have changed. Updated November 2026.
