Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This policy explains what cookies hijodelmar.cl uses, why we use them, and how you can control them. By continuing to browse the site, or by clicking "Accept" on the cookie banner, you consent to the use of cookies as described here.

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that a website saves to your browser when you visit it. Cookies allow the site to remember information about your visit, making it easier to return and more useful overall.

Cookies we use

This site uses only Google Analytics (GA4) analytics cookies. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, or social media third-party cookies.

Cookie Provider Purpose Duration
_ga Google Analytics Distinguishes unique users. Allows counting how many people visit the site. 2 years
_ga_* Google Analytics Stores the current session state (pages viewed, time on site). 2 years
hdm_cookie_consent hijodelmar.cl Saves your consent preference so we don't ask you again. Until you clear localStorage

Information collected by Google Analytics is used solely to understand how visitors use the site (most visited pages, traffic source, devices). No personally identifiable data is shared with third parties.

Your consent

The first time you visit the site, a banner appears at the bottom where you can accept or decline analytics cookies. If you decline, Google Analytics is not activated and no tracking cookies are placed in your browser.

How to disable cookies from your browser

In addition to using the buttons above, you can control and delete cookies directly from your browser settings:

Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
Firefox: Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
Edge: Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Cookies and site permissions.

Note that blocking all cookies may affect the functionality of some websites.
