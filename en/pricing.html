Services & Pricing

Five types of nautical advisory. Each one adapts to your situation, your vessel and your experience level. Pricing available on request.

Why no fixed rates? Because every advisory is different: it depends on the complexity of your question, the length of the session and what you actually need. A fair, personalised price is offered for each case. Write and get a reply within minutes.

We analyse your route together before you set sail: weather, wind, tides, currents, anchorages and ports along the leg; narrow passages and risk points; estimated times, fuel consumption and a contingency plan for bad weather. All tailored to your vessel type and crew level.
