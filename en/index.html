About me

30 years at sea, at your service

Welcome — my name is Rodrigo Troncoso, I'm a Captain. My story starts in commercial fishing, sailing Chile's open ocean and southern fjords all the way to the Antarctic Sea. The Drake Passage and Cape Horn became my home, and that experience — witnessing nature at its most powerful — prepared me for everything that followed.

From there I made the leap into offshore work, as Captain of highly specialized vessels: PSVs, AHTSs, dive support ships and FSIVs, operating for the oil industry off the coasts of Angola, Brazil, Congo, Ghana, Venezuela, Trinidad and Tobago, Cameroon and the Caribbean — maneuvering in open sea just meters from oil platforms in waters that forgive no mistakes.

I have over 15 years of DP (dynamic positioning) operations, with current international certifications, all carried out with the composure and clear head that only comes from commanding in any weather, any port, any emergency. I returned to Chile with all of that knowledge, and now I sail the Patagonian fjords again in wellboats for the salmon industry — but now, in this stage of my life, with a new purpose.

Today I put all of this experience at your disposal: for those who sail or want to sail, sports enthusiasts, adventurers, business people, or anyone who wants to move through the sea with me as their co-pilot. Just 30 years of experience — and I'm still evolving. Get in touch.
