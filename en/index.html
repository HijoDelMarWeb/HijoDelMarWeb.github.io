About me

30 years at sea, at your service

Commercial fishing · Antarctic Sea · Cape Horn

My story starts in commercial fishing, sailing Chile's open ocean and southern fjords all the way to the Antarctic Sea. The Drake Passage and Cape Horn became my home — witnessing nature at its most powerful, and gaining the foundation that prepared me for everything that followed.

Offshore · Angola, Brazil, Ghana, Venezuela and more

From there I made the leap into offshore work, as Captain of highly specialized vessels — PSVs, AHTSs, dive support ships and FSIVs — operating for the oil industry off the coasts of Angola, Brazil, Congo, Ghana, Venezuela, Trinidad and Tobago, Cameroon and the Caribbean. Maneuvering in open sea just meters from oil platforms, in waters that forgive no mistakes.

Dynamic positioning · +15 years · DP certifications

Over 15 years of DP operations with current international certifications — all carried out with the composure that only comes from commanding in any weather, any port, any emergency. I returned to Chile with all of that knowledge, and now sail the Patagonian fjords again in wellboats, but with a new purpose.

Today · Chile · At your disposal

Today I put all of this experience at your disposal: for those who sail or want to sail, sports enthusiasts, adventurers, business people — anyone who wants to move through the sea with a real professional as their co-pilot. 30 years of experience. The sea never stops teaching, and neither do I. Get in touch.
